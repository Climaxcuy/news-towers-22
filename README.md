# News Towers — Sunday 22 November 2026

**News Towers** is a one-page newspaper for a single day — Sunday
22 November 2026 — drawn as a city of towers: a night skyline for the
Las Vegas Grand Prix, a launch tower for Falcon 9 NROL-136 at
Vandenberg, and stadium floodlight towers for Liverpool v Manchester
United at Anfield. The date itself is the masthead; the towers are the
paper's scene device, kept inside the stories where they belong.

The page carries 40 stories, every one an event of 22 November 2026
taken from a verified same-day pool:

- **3 lead scenes** — Las Vegas GP (04:00 UTC, 50 laps, round 22),
  Falcon 9 NROL-136 (window 12:16–12:46 UTC, booster B1097 to LZ4),
  and Liverpool v Manchester United (16:30 UTC, Matchweek 11).
- **10 more pictured cards** — the IRGC's claimed Bahrain radar strike,
  the Houthi missile intercepted toward Makkah, COP31's final text at
  Antalya, Myanmar's M4.1 quake, India's record 720/9 at Wellington,
  the Alcaraz v Zverev Turin final, the LPGA CME finale, the Kyushu
  sumo finale, the rain-moved RSM Classic round 3, and NFL Week 12.
- **27 briefs**, a six-number day strip, a six-slot UTC timeline, a
  real-polygon world map with 13 numbered pins, and a DATE CHECK box
  naming eight dated stories deliberately left out (plus one that has
  not happened yet: the Davis Cup final, 29 November).

Every card carries its source or certainty tag, and every deck carries
at least two specific facts. One red accent runs the numbers, the hook
and the DATE CHECK; everything else stays on light cream newsprint.

## Running

`python3 main.py` copies the committed `entry.png` into the directory
named by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`. To rebuild the artwork from source,
run `python3 draw.py` (requires Pillow; fonts and map polygon data are
bundled in this repo).
